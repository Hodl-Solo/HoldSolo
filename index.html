Rabbit Holes

I’m Simon James. HodlSolo on Nostr, Twitter/X and a few other places.

I spent most of my working life in music and engineering, then gradually found myself spending more time around Bitcoin: starting meetups, connecting organisers, onboarding businesses and building tools for the UK community.

HodlSolo.com is the permanent home for that work — and for the other things I make when curiosity pulls me somewhere else.
